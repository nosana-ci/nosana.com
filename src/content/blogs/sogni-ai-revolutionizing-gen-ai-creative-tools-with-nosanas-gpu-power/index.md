## The Power of Nosana's Decentralized GPUs

Initially, Sogni relied on users' local devices for processing, including Apple's M1, M2, and M3 chips. While capable, these devices struggled with the computational demands of **Stable Diffusion models**, leading to delays that disrupted the creative flow. Sogni needed a **scalable, cost-effective, and reliable solution.**

Nosana provided the perfect answer. Its **decentralized GPU network** taps into global consumer hardware, enabling flexible and affordable GPU sourcing. By integrating Nosana, Sogni offloaded intensive computational tasks to remote GPUs, delivering **faster, smoother art generation** without sacrificing quality or affordability.

To ensure low inference times and a seamless user experience, Nosana recommended the **NVIDIA RTX 4090** for Sogni. This GPU strikes the perfect balance between cost and performance, with specifications such as:

- **CUDA Cores:** 16,384
- **Boost Clock:** 2.52 GHz
- **Memory:** 24 GB GDDR6X

For larger-scale applications, Nosana also offers GPUs like the NVIDIA H100 or NVIDIA A100, which are ideal for training AI models.
