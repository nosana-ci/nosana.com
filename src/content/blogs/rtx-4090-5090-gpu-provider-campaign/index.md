Demand for high performance AI compute on Nosana is growing fast. Developers are using RTX 4090 and RTX 5090 GPUs to power demanding workloads across video generation, large language models, multimodal inference, and other advanced AI applications. Demand has now grown beyond the capacity currently available in these markets, creating a clear opportunity to expand the network.

That is where GPU providers come in. If you own an RTX 4090 or RTX 5090 with available capacity, you can connect it to Nosana and make that compute accessible to developers, researchers, and businesses around the world. Your GPU can support real AI workloads while generating earnings whenever customer jobs are assigned to your host.

To accelerate this expansion, Nosana is launching a two month campaign for eligible RTX 4090 and RTX 5090 hosts. For every qualifying day, participating hosts will receive an additional incentive equivalent to 20% daily utilization, on top of any earnings generated from actual customer workloads.

Whether you are joining Nosana for the first time or already provide compute to the network, this is an opportunity to put more of your GPU capacity to work while helping Nosana meet the growing demand for high performance AI infrastructure.

## Receive the Equivalent of 20% Daily Utilization

For every qualifying day, eligible RTX 4090 and RTX 5090 hosts will receive an additional incentive equivalent to 20% daily utilization.

This means your host is rewarded as if it had completed 4.8 hours of paid work during a 24 hour period, provided it meets the daily uptime requirement. Any earnings generated from actual customer workloads are added on top of this incentive.

The campaign is open to both new providers and existing Nosana hosts with eligible hardware. To qualify, your host must be registered, online and maintain at least 85% uptime each day. Hosts that meet or exceed this threshold receive the full daily incentive. If uptime falls below 85%, no incentive is awarded for that day.

## Why Nosana Needs More GPU Providers

RTX 4090 and RTX 5090 GPUs provide the performance and memory required for demanding AI applications, including image and video generation, large language models and multimodal inference.

Customer demand for this capacity currently exceeds the available supply on Nosana. Adding more reliable GPUs will allow more teams to deploy and scale their applications while giving hardware owners access to a global market for their available compute capacity.

Nosana handles workload scheduling, orchestration and payments. Providers retain control of their hardware and can monitor performance, workload activity and earnings through the host dashboard.

## Estimate Your Potential Earnings

Your potential earnings depend on your GPU, how many hours you make it available and the customer workloads it receives. The Nosana GPU Provider page includes an earnings calculator that allows you to select your hardware and estimate your potential monthly earnings based on your available capacity.

The calculator estimates your regular workload earnings. The additional campaign incentive is calculated separately and depends on meeting the 85% daily uptime requirement.

## Access to Premium Markets

Hosts that meet Nosana’s technical, performance and reliability requirements can be promoted to Premium markets.

Premium markets connect customers with dependable, high quality GPU capacity. Maintaining strong uptime and consistent performance can therefore provide access to additional workload opportunities during and after the incentive period.

## Who Can Participate?

You can participate if:

- You provide an NVIDIA RTX 4090 or RTX 5090.
- Your GPU is registered and verified on the Nosana network.
- Your host is online and meets Nosana’s technical and performance requirements.
- Your host maintains at least 85% uptime each day.
- Space remains available within the campaign.

Existing Nosana hosts are also eligible as long as their hardware and daily uptime meet these requirements.

## How to Start Providing GPU Compute on Nosana

Getting your GPU connected to Nosana takes four steps:

1. **Install the Nosana node.** Set up the node software and connect your GPU to the network.
2. **Register and verify your hardware.** Complete registration and confirm that your GPU meets the required specifications.
3. **Bring your host online.** Make your GPU available to receive customer workloads and begin generating earnings.
4. **Track your performance.** Monitor uptime, workload activity and payments through the host dashboard.

Once your eligible RTX 4090 or RTX 5090 is registered and online, it can qualify for the campaign incentive by maintaining at least 85% daily uptime.

You can find the earnings calculator, hardware requirements, setup instructions and access to the host dashboard on the [Nosana GPU Provider page](https://nosana.com/gpu-providers/).
