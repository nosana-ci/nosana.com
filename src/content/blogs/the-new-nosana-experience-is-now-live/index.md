Our redesigned website is now live \- built to make using, exploring, and contributing to Nosana clearer, faster, and more intuitive than ever before🚀

This update is not just cosmetic. It reflects how the network has evolved and how our community actually uses Nosana today: as builders running workloads, as hosts providing compute, and as contributors shaping a growing decentralized AI ecosystem.

**Here’s What’s New**

## **Instantly claim $50 in free GPU credits — directly on the site**

One of the most important upgrades is now available from the homepage.

You can now **claim $50 in free GPU credits instantly on the Nosana website.** No extra steps, no waiting — just immediate access to compute you can start using right away.

This makes it easier than ever for new builders to experiment, test, and ship on Nosana without friction.

If you'd like a quick walkthrough, watch [this short guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J9lfl2QOmhU) showing how to claim your credits and run your first workload.

**A clearer, more useful Nosana website**

Beyond credits, the new site introduces a range of informational and usability improvements designed to make your experience smoother and more practical:

- A redesigned structure that makes it easier to understand Nosana’s products and network
- A built-in calculator to estimate your **approximate GPU spend** before running workloads
- Better visibility into **host earnings and network performance**
- A centralized place to watch all Nosana **live sessions and recordings**
- A cleaner, more intuitive overview of how the network works and what it enables

In short: the website is now a much stronger starting point, whether you are a first-time visitor or a long-time user.

**Purpose-built web apps — clearer roles, simpler workflows**

Alongside the website refresh, we’ve refined our core web apps to make each of them more focused and easier to navigate.

### **A dedicated hub for [GPU hosts](https://host.nosana.com/)**

If you provide compute to the network (or want to), you now have a single home.

At [**host.nosana.com**](http://host.nosana.com) you can:

- Track your host performance and earnings in one place
- Monitor workloads, uptime, and rewards
- Follow clear guides on how to become a Nosana host

This makes hosting more transparent, accessible, and professional

[**Live network insights**](http://explore.nosana.com)

For anyone who wants to understand what’s happening on Nosana in real time, our network explorer gives you direct visibility.

At [**explore.nosana.com**](https://explore.nosana.com/) you can track:

- Completed workloads
- Currently running workloads
- Total compute hours delivered by the network

It’s a live window into Nosana’s activity and growth 🌱

**From Dashboard to [Deploy](http://deploy.nosana.com)**

What was previously the Nosana dashboard has evolved into a dedicated **Deploy page**.

At [**deploy.nosana.com**](http://deploy.nosana.com) you can now:

- **Claim your $50 in free GPU credits** to start experimenting on the network
- Launch GPU workloads more easily
- Select and use Nosana GPUs through a clearer interface
- Get started faster with streamlined workflows

This redesign is all about reducing friction and helping you move from idea to execution faster.

**Getting started with your free credits**

To help new users get up to speed, we’ve also published [a short tutorial video](https://youtu.be/J9lfl2QOmhU?si=NdrhNmj6NGj0MVS9) that walks through:

- How to claim your $50 in free GPU credits
- How to run your first GPU workload on Nosana

You can claim your credits directly from the Nosana homepage and follow along with the guide to get started in minutes.

**What this means for Nosana**

This launch represents a more mature, accessible, and builder-friendly Nosana.

The network itself continues to grow, but now, your way of interacting with it is simpler, clearer, and more powerful.

Whether you’re running AI workloads, hosting GPUs, or just exploring decentralized compute, the new Nosana experience is designed with you in mind.
