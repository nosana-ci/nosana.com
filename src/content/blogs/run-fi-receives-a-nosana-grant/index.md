Nosana has awarded a grant to Run-Fi, a fintech project building an AI-powered platform to help businesses manage payments across traditional banks and blockchains. Run-Fi will use Nosana's decentralized GPU network to power its AI technology, with the goal of making financial transactions easier to track, verify, and manage.

As more businesses adopt stablecoins and blockchain-based payments, keeping track of money moving between different financial systems is becoming increasingly complicated. Banks and blockchains often record the same transaction differently, leaving finance teams investigating discrepancies and confirming that payments have reached their destination. Run-Fi is working to change that by using AI to automatically match transactions, identify inconsistencies, and help businesses resolve payment issues without relying on time-consuming manual processes. With Nosana providing the computing power behind its AI models, Run-Fi aims to make this technology more accessible and cost-efficient for fintech companies and financial institutions.

## Making Payments Between Banks and Blockchains Easier

Imagine a business sending a payment through a traditional bank and receiving it through a blockchain-based system. Although the money has moved successfully, the transaction may appear differently in each system, with different references, timestamps, or amounts due to currency conversions. For businesses processing thousands of transactions, checking these differences manually can quickly become a costly and time-consuming task.

Run-Fi is building a platform that handles much of this work automatically. It starts by matching payments using information such as amounts, dates, and transaction references. When the details don't line up, AI helps identify possible matches and investigate discrepancies, flagging only the cases that need human attention. The platform also keeps a record of how each transaction was matched, giving finance teams a clear view of their payments and making it easier to review financial activity. Run-Fi's long-term goal is to automate at least 85% of transaction reconciliation, allowing businesses to spend less time checking records and more time focusing on their operations.

## Why Run-Fi Is Building with Nosana

AI plays an important role in Run-Fi's approach, particularly when transactions cannot be matched using standard rules. In these cases, AI models help recognize similarities between different payment records, while language models analyze more complicated situations and suggest how they should be resolved. Running these models requires GPU computing power, which is where Nosana comes in.

Run-Fi plans to use Nosana as its primary AI inference provider, accessing decentralized GPU resources to run the models behind its transaction-matching and resolution processes. Instead of depending entirely on traditional centralized AI services, Run-Fi will be able to run open-weight models on Nosana's network, giving the team greater flexibility over its AI infrastructure. This approach is designed to help Run-Fi manage computing costs as transaction volumes grow, while supporting the development of a financial platform that can handle increasingly complex workloads.

For Nosana, the collaboration introduces another practical application for decentralized GPU computing, demonstrating how its infrastructure can support AI-powered financial services alongside other types of AI applications.

> "Money now moves across cards, bank transfers, mobile money, stablecoins and, increasingly, software agents, and each rail keeps its own record. Knowing what actually happened to a payment is getting harder. Run-Fi starts with reconciliation because everything else depends on it. Nosana gives us a modular, affordable way to run that work as we grow."
>
> — **Allan Mang'eni, Founder of Run-Fi**

## What's Next for Run-Fi?

Founded by Allan Mang'eni in Nairobi, Kenya, Run-Fi has already developed several core components of its platform, including its transaction-matching system, blockchain integration, and tools for tracking payment activity. With support from the Nosana grant, the team will focus on connecting these components into a complete solution and bringing its AI-powered services onto Nosana's GPU network.

The next steps include deploying and testing Run-Fi's AI models on Nosana, onboarding its first enterprise customer or pilot user, and processing real financial transactions through the platform. The grant will provide GPU credits to support development, testing, and early production workloads, alongside technical guidance and opportunities for collaboration with the Nosana team. As the project progresses, Run-Fi and Nosana also plan to publish a technical case study sharing the results of the integration, including how decentralized GPU computing performs in a real-world financial application.

With more financial services adopting blockchain technology, Run-Fi is exploring how AI can help bridge the gap between traditional banking and onchain payments, while Nosana provides the computing infrastructure to support that development.
